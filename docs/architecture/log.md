| 2026-10-22 | Clarified the intro lead in the `MS-18-animated-header-title` concept (MS-18, D4/D6). This fixes a contradiction found by the tester. `useTypewriter.type(leadDelay)` with `leadDelay > 0` keeps the phase `idle` during the lead, so the cursor blinks while waiting. The lead timer is the single pending timer, and `erase()`/`stop()` cancel it. When it fires, the phase becomes `typing` and the next character follows after `typeDelays[count]`. Intro: idle 0–0.80 s, typing (solid cursor) from 0.80 s, `m` at 1.00 s. `type()` without a lead (retype on pointer leave) is `typing` at once. Chosen over a separate lead timer in `useBrandTitle`, to keep the single-timer cancellation in one place. |
